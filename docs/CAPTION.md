## English

> Rapperswil-Jona, 1919 – 2026 🇨🇭 – 5,356 buildings from the Swiss federal building
> register, each appearing in the year it was built.
>
> In 1918, 680 of today's buildings stood – Rapperswil's old town and the villages around
> it. Four in five went up after 1945; the 1970s and 1980s alone added 1,524.
>
> ℹ️ Year = construction year in the federal register (GWR). For almost half of the
> buildings since 1919 the register only knows the construction period; their year is
> estimated from neighbouring buildings of the same period – faded bars. Grey = no date.
> ℹ️ Data: BFS – Federal Register of Buildings and Dwellings · swisstopo – swissBUILDINGS3D
> 3.0, swissALTIRegio · WSL/FOEN – NFI vegetation height model
> ℹ️ Tools: Python (Claude), custom 3D renderer
>
> #rapperswiljona #rapperswil #lakezurich #switzerland #urbanism #map #dataviz #gis
